**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **Borderless window: the taskbar comes up.** Windows decides whether to keep the taskbar away by asking whether the window in front is a full-screen app, and a window that covers the screen without a title bar is one, two pixels short or not. A Borderless window is now what Claude's and Blender's windows are when they fill the screen: a window Windows maximizes, with a title bar it never draws. Windows doesn't take it for a full-screen app, so the taskbar stays in front and comes up when the pointer goes to its edge, and there are no more two pixels showing at the bottom.
- **Full screen** stays a full-screen app: it covers the whole screen and the taskbar doesn't come up.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.21-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.21.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
