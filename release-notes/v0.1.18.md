**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Ctrl+S takes the page full screen.** It used to hide the tabs and leave an empty strip and a border around the page. Now the tabs go and the page fills the screen, as Full screen or Borderless window says in Settings; Ctrl+S again brings the tabs and the window back as they were. Full screen you put on yourself (F11, a video) is left alone. **Settings › Appearance › Ctrl+S goes full screen** turns it off, and Ctrl+S then only hides the tabs as before.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.18-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.18.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
