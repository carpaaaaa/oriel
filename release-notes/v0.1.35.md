**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Drag your bookmarks where you want them.** In the bookmarks bar and in the bookmarks list, drag a bookmark before or after another, or drop it on a folder to put it inside. The tiles of a new tab follow the order of your Home folder, so this is also how you put them in order.
- **The bookmarks bar has a right-click menu**: Open, Open in New Tab, Edit…, Move to, Remove; for a folder Rename…, New Folder Inside…, Move to, Remove. The list's menu gains Open in New Tab too.
- **Home stays off the bar.** While Settings › Tabs › Show the Home bookmarks on a new tab is on (as it comes since 0.1.34), the Home folder no longer sits in the bookmarks bar: its sites are on every new tab already. Show on New Tab, in a bookmark's menu, puts it there.
- **The Home folder makes itself.** Turning on Show the Home bookmarks on a new tab makes the Home folder if you don't have one, and turning it off takes it away again if it is still empty. Show on New Tab makes it too.
- **A Settings button, if you want one.** Settings › Appearance › Tabs › Show the Settings button puts a gear up at the top right, beside the bookmarks button, so Settings is always one click away.

## Fixed

- **Back to a floating video's tab, smoothly.** Going back to the tab a floating video came from showed the video stretched over the whole page for a moment, then a jump. The page now comes back in its own colour, lays itself out, and fades in.
- **Links from other apps with Oriel closed.** With Open links from other apps in a small window on, a link that started Oriel could open the browser window over the small one, as if the link had been lost. Now only the small window opens, and Open in Oriel works even with no Oriel window open: the window comes back with your tabs and the page joins them. Turned off, links from other apps open in a tab, as the setting now says.
- **No more cut letters in the bookmarks bar.** The tails of g, j, p, q and y were cut off in the bar's names, and in the bookmarks list.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.35-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.35.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
