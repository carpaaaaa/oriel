**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Ads blocked as uBlock Origin blocks them.** Oriel's blocker now uses uBlock Origin's own lists (uBlock filters, EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Peter Lowe's list), updated every day, with its scripts for YouTube and for sites that fight blockers. A tab's menu can stop blocking on one site.
- **Oriel as your default browser in one click.** Oriel now tells Windows it is a browser. Make Oriel the Default opens Oriel's own page in Windows Settings, where Set default is the only click left. Links and .html files from other apps then open in Oriel.
- **Bookmark, extensions and downloads at the top right**, as on the Mac. The extensions button shows once you have an extension; downloads while a file comes in, or always with Always show.
- **Settings › Extensions** lists your extensions, each with its switch, Runs on, pin, options and Remove.
- **Keyboard & Mouse › Shortcuts**: give any command the key you like.
- **New tab in a floating field** works as in Search: Ctrl+T raises the field over the page, and the tab opens only when you press Enter.
- **Site shortcuts** as in Search: a word of your own, like yt, and an address with %s where your words go. No ready-made sites; change or delete yours.
- **Smooth scrolling** with the mouse wheel, on to begin with, and Ctrl+wheel zoom that glides from one level to the next.
- **Smoother motion** on the Mac's springs: closing and opening tabs, the address shown in a tab, the column hidden and shown with Ctrl+S.
- **Oriel's icon fills its square** like other Windows apps, and the taskbar's previews show Oriel's icon instead of Electron's.

## Fixed

- Full screen from a maximized window, by F11 or by a video, covers the whole screen and comes back maximized. Leaving a video's full screen no longer leaves the window as big as the screen, or the video full screen inside the window. Keys work again right after the full-screen buttons, and Win+arrows in full screen no longer break it.
- Alt+F4 on the last window asks first when Confirm before quitting is on.
- High contrast text works: soft grey titles to begin with, as on the Mac, and ink with the switch.
- The loading line with the tabs hidden takes the text's colour.
- Translation languages is a clean list, with Add Language.
- The Web Store bar's Remove stays inside it, and the extension's name shows again.
- Letters like g and p are no longer cut at the bottom in Settings.
- The keys with Alt+Shift (Passwords, Copy Clean Link, Split) work.
- Prevent cross-site tracking also stops frames from another site reading their cookies.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.4-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.4.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
