**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Passwords from Chrome in one press.** Chrome, Edge and Brave lock their passwords to themselves, so Oriel can't read them on its own. In Bring things over, choose the browser and press **Start**: it opens at its own Export page, you press Download file and confirm with Windows Hello, and Oriel picks the file up as soon as it lands in Downloads, Desktop or Documents, brings the passwords in and sends the file to the Recycle Bin. Cancel, or closing the panel, stops the waiting.

## Fixed

- **Names that only exist with www open.** A site like unibg.it has no address without www, so typing it ended in "No site at that address". Oriel now tries the other name once, with www in front or without it, keeping the path and the port. If both fail, the page says the name you typed. Links inside pages are left as they are.
- **Hide YouTube Shorts and Scroll bars are in Settings.** Both rows were missing: Hide YouTube Shorts (Browsing, Video) and Scroll bars (Browsing, Pages) now show, and Find a setting finds them.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.24-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.24.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
