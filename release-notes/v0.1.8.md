**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Two pages side by side by carrying a tab onto the page.** Drag a tab from the column over the page: two bands show at its edges. Rest in one to see the page as it would be, let go there and the tab stands beside the page, on that side. The divider between them changes their widths. Letting go in the middle does nothing.
- **See through the floating video** (Settings › Video): away from the pointer the floating video lets what is behind show through, as much as the slider says. With the pointer on it, it's solid again. Oriel's window and picture in picture both, also a picture in picture opened by the site's own button.
- **The bookmarks bar** (Settings › Appearance › Show the bookmarks bar): your bookmarks in a row right above the page, always there while it's on. A folder opens its list under it, and what doesn't fit is behind ».

## Fixed

- **Restore Down after full screen no longer closes Oriel.** Maximized, then F11 and back, then Restore Down took the whole app down.
- **A window closed maximized comes back to its own size** when you restore it after a restart, instead of a small window in the middle.
- **Maximized or full screen, the window's buttons stay on the screen.** Oriel checks that the window is inside the screen and puts it back if Windows ever leaves it past an edge.
- **A page that is still loading is dark in a dark Oriel**, not a white card, until the site draws.
- **The X on a tab closes all the tabs you picked** with Ctrl-click or Shift-click, as Ctrl+W does.
- **Closing picture in picture with its own button** is noticed, so leaving Oriel floats the next video on its own again.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.8-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.8.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
