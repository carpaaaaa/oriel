**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **The floating video shows with Oriel maximized.** Switching tabs or Ctrl+Shift+P floated the video, but with the window maximized the little window went under Oriel and nobody saw it: only "Playing in the floating window" was left in the tab. It now stays on top of everything.

## Changed

- **Less memory while Oriel is open.** A tab out of sight for half a minute, and the interface of a minimized window, let their caches go (a Wikipedia page in the background drops by about a third). With Windows short of memory, tabs left alone sleep after 2 minutes, whatever the pace in Settings; pinned tabs, ones playing sound, downloading or holding something typed stay awake as always.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.28-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.28.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
