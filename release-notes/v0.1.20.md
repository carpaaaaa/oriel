**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **Ctrl+S is full screen, edge to edge, everywhere.** With Borderless window chosen it came out as a window with a strip of buttons on top and a border round the page. Now the page fills the screen whatever the style, with the tabs coming down at the top as in full screen, and the window still leaves the taskbar's edge, so the taskbar comes up when the pointer goes there. It works with the tabs across the top or down the side, and also when you are already in full screen.
- **Leaving full screen** (Full screen style, from a window that wasn't maximized) left the window as big as the screen; it now comes back to the size it had. This slipped into 0.1.19.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.20-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.20.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
