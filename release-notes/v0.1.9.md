**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **A tab carried onto another tab puts them side by side.** In the column, rest the tab you're carrying on another one for a moment: it's marked, and letting go splits the two. Keep moving and the tabs make room as before.
- **Full screen or borderless window** (Settings › Appearance › Full screen): Borderless window fills the whole screen and keeps the window as it is when maximized, with its buttons always there.
- **Hide YouTube Shorts** (Settings › Video): no Shorts shelves, button or results on YouTube, and a Shorts link opens as an ordinary video.
- **Copy on sites that won't let you** (Settings › Pages › Let me copy on every site, or right-click a tab › Allow Copying on This Site): selecting, copying and right-clicking work again where a site stops them.

## Fixed

- **The address you type in a tab across the top** no longer has the tails of g, p and y cut off, and the tab widens only as much as the address needs.
- **Settings' ticks** for Back, forward and reload and for the buttons under the column now look like the rest of Settings, and Tabs give when pressed has its hand back.
- **Reading mode** shows in Settings with its key, Ctrl+Shift+R, so finding "reading" finds it.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.9-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.9.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
