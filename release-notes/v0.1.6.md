**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **A video's full screen is the video alone.** YouTube's, a video embedded in another site's, F11's: the page now covers the whole screen, with no tab strip, column or card around it, and moving the pointer to the top no longer brings a strip of buttons over the video. Leaving puts the window back as it was, with its buttons.
- **Things floating over the page stay in place** when the window goes full screen, maximizes or comes back: the question to save a password no longer slides off the screen's left.
- **The saved accounts' list goes away** when you leave the sign-in without picking one, also when the page changes its address by itself, as Google's sign-in does.
- **Extensions brought over from another browser are installed**, one at a time, each asking first. They used to be counted and then never came. Those from another browser's own store, like Opera's add-ons, aren't in the Chrome Web Store as they are: Bring things over now names them, so you can look for them there.
- **A turned-off extension's menu opens**, with Remove, without turning it on first.
- **The tab's speaker** is drawn as the Mac's, with thin waves apart from the speaker, and the crossed-out one when muted.
- **The + beside the tabs across the top** no longer keeps a grey ring around it after a click, and lights up in the tabs' own grey.
- **Bring things over's Done** is a button like the Mac's, with its cross drawn instead of a letter, and Escape closes the panel.
- **Site shortcuts** say that the address starts with https:// or http://.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.6-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.6.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
