**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Ctrl+Tab as on the Mac, and as Alt+Tab.** A quick Ctrl+Tab goes straight to the tab you used before, and again takes you back. Hold Ctrl and the switcher comes up with every page of the space, each with a picture of it: Tab and Shift+Tab move along, the arrows too, and letting go of Ctrl opens the one you're on. A click or Enter opens a card, Esc puts the switcher away, and Ctrl+W, Ctrl+R and Ctrl+M close, reload or mute the card you're on.
- **Installing is one click.** Open the setup and Oriel installs for you, without questions, and opens when it's done.

## Fixed

- With the ad blocker on, YouTube no longer stalls for seconds at a time.
- A quick Ctrl+Tab left the switcher open instead of going back a tab, and letting go of Ctrl after Ctrl+W in the switcher wasn't noticed.
- **Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 switch spaces** works: with it on, Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 pick spaces and Alt+1 to Alt+9 pick tabs, and the space button, its menu, Settings and Shortcuts say so. Before, the switch changed nothing.
- **Show tab numbers while Ctrl is held** follows the keys: with the tabs on Alt+1 to Alt+9, holding Alt shows the numbers. Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 now pick the tabs in the order the numbers show.
- Alt with the keypad's digits types a character again (Alt+0233 for é) instead of switching spaces.
- **Bounce at the top and bottom** works: with it on, a page stretches past its ends under a precision touchpad and springs back, as on a Mac's trackpad. Before, the switch changed nothing on Windows.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.5-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.5.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

If an earlier Oriel was installed for everyone on this PC (in Program Files), this one installs for you alone beside it: remove the other one in Settings › Apps.
