**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **Crash when the window changed size or state** (going full screen, back to a window, maximizing again), most of all with borderless full screen. Oriel asked Windows to change the window, and while Windows was still answering it asked again from inside the first call; Windows' own window tools such as Windhawk's mods make that happen every time, and the app went down. It never asks from inside a call now: a question gets the last answer, an order waits until the first call has finished. Checked here with the very mods that crashed it: full screen, borderless, back to a window and maximize again, over and over, no crash.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.17-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.17.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
