**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **Borderless window and the taskbar.** With the taskbar set to hide, moving the pointer to its edge now brings it up: the window leaves two pixels at that edge, as Windows does for a maximized window, where one wasn't enough.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.14-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.14.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
