**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- Leaving Oriel for another app while the Ctrl+Tab switcher was up could bring Oriel back in front.
- The page that comes to the front with Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9, Ctrl+Page Down, a pin, a tab clicked in the column or the switcher's Enter has the keys at once: what you type, the arrow keys and the next shortcut go to it.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.7-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.7.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
