**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Changed

- **Ctrl+S hides the tabs and the page fills the whole window, and nothing more.** It no longer goes full screen (the "Ctrl+S goes full screen" setting is gone). In a window or maximized, the page now reaches the window's edges: no strip left empty above it, no border round it. The window's buttons come down at the top when the pointer touches the top edge; with the tabs across the top they come down with the tab strip, and the bookmark button with them. Ctrl+S again brings the tabs back and the page as it was.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.26-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.26.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
