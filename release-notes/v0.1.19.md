**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **Borderless window and the taskbar, again.** On Windows the window now covers the screen by itself in both styles, and no longer goes through Electron's own full screen, whose leave-and-enter on a change of style could leave the window as it was. **Full screen** covers the whole screen and keeps the taskbar away; **Borderless window** leaves two pixels on the taskbar's edge, so the taskbar comes up when the pointer goes there. Changing one for the other inside full screen is a resize at once, with no leave and enter. A video's own full screen stays full screen whole.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.19-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.19.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
