**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **Sign in with Google works.** "Continue with Google" (on Pinterest and on every site with Google's button) did nothing: Google's script asks the browser for a newer way to sign in (FedCM) first, and Oriel had nothing behind it to answer, so the request waited for ever. That way is switched off now, and Google opens its own sign-in window, as it does in any browser that doesn't have it.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.31-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.31.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
