**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Changed

- **The card after an update asks for a star or a coffee.** Buy me a coffee (buymeacoffee.com/charstudio) takes the place of Don't ask again, next to Star on GitHub. Either one opens its page and ends the card for good; the X, or leaving it alone, puts it away until the next update.
- **The card is centred and clean at every text size.** It is as big as its content, with the same room above and below, its buttons ending where its padding does, and the star and the X on the title's line, from 100% to 140% text. Nothing empty is left over the page.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.30-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.30.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
