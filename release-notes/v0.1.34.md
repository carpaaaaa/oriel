**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own. This one brings over what Search 1.0.20, 1.0.21 and 1.0.22 added on the Mac.

## New

- **A site that couldn't be found for a moment loads by itself.** When the name of a site doesn't come back (the Wi-Fi changing, a VPN coming up), the page says "Couldn't find this site. Trying again…" and tries again after 1, 3 and 8 seconds, and at once when the network comes back; a tab you aren't looking at tries once you go to it. A form is never sent again, nor an address typed without `https://` to a site you've never been to. Messages for pages that fail to load now show at all: they were hidden under a blank page.
- **Paste and Go.** Right-click any address field (the field over a page, a new tab's, the address in the tab, the field raised from any app) for Undo, Cut, Copy, Paste, Paste and Go when the clipboard holds an address, and Select All. They had no menu before.
- **A speaker on pins that play sound.** A pinned square whose page plays sound wears a small speaker in its corner, in the column and across the top; a click mutes it, another unmutes it.
- **What's playing lists every tab playing out of sight**, one row each, newest on top, pinned tabs and tabs in other spaces included. A player paused from there keeps its row and plays again from where it was.
- **Export Spaces…** in Settings › General › Bring things over: every space to a bookmarks page any browser imports, or to Search's own spaces file, which Bring things over › File… reads back as spaces, on a PC or a Mac.
- **Developer Mode, site by site.** A page on this PC (localhost, 127.0.0.1, .localhost, .test) shows a slim bar with its whole address, Developer Tools, Reload Ignoring Cache and a switch that keeps the cache off for the site. Any site can be put in it from its tab's menu or with Shift+F12. F12 and Ctrl+Shift+I open Developer Tools.
- **Hide Elements takes every element like the one you point at**: hold Alt and the outline says how many are like it, then Alt-click hides them all, on later visits too. A plain click still hides just the one, and now hides exactly that one, also on pages that build themselves after they load.
- **The floating video works on any site's player**, not only streaming sites; Don't Float Videos Here is in the tab's menu.
- **Double click to rename**, in Settings › Tabs: a click on the tab you're on opens its name to type over instead of its address.
- **Drag a finished download** out of the Downloads list as its file, onto a page or into File Explorer.
- **Show the bookmarks bar only on new tabs**, in Settings › Appearance › Tabs.
- **Copy captures only**, in Settings › Browsing › Pages: a capture goes to the clipboard and no PNG is saved.
- **A custom search engine can have a suggestions address**, in Settings › Browsing › Search with.
- **A mouse's sideways wheel** goes through the spaces, one space a turn.

## Changed

- **On by default now**, for everyone who never touched the switch as well as for new installs, and each can still be turned off in Settings: Try HTTPS first, links opened in front go beside their page, address bar commands, Ctrl+Z reopens a closed tab, swipe back closes a tab a link opened, reading mode follows light and dark, Capture pages, your own style for a site, the Home bookmarks on a new tab, save power with Energy Saver, minimize the floating video, folders in pinned rows, what's playing in other tabs, counts on pins, and Leave a new tab when the last tab closes.
- **Tabs and pins show the site's icon** instead of a letter; a site without an icon still shows its letter. Settings › Appearance › Tabs › Tabs show brings the letters back.
- **The loading line** along the page's top shows whenever a page loads, with the tabs in sight too; its switch is gone.
- **Find a setting takes the keys** as Settings opens, so a setting's name can be typed at once, and keeps its words when you come back to the tab. Escape empties it.
- **Tabs you aren't looking at load a few at a time**, so twenty tabs reloading at once no longer stall the window; the tab on screen never waits.
- **Return takes the top suggestion** (Settings › Browsing) picks it as you type, in every address field.
- Back, forward and reload with nowhere to go are dimmed to half rather than nearly gone. The edge that brings a hidden sidebar is wider and easier to catch, and the column's edge holds its cursor while you pull it.
- An address you type that sends you to another site (twitter.com to x.com) finishes from its own first letters next time. A site shortcut takes an address typed without `https://`.

## Fixed

- **With Icons only, a click on the tab you're on no longer closes it.** Its close button now waits half a second and sits small in the tile's corner; every tile's tooltip says where its page is.
- Closing the last tab leaves a new, empty tab instead of taking you into a pinned tab, and Ctrl+W no longer goes back and forth for ever between a pin and a new tab.
- A Chrome theme from the Chrome Web Store is no longer installed only to change nothing: Oriel says themes aren't supported and where its own colours are.
- With the bookmarks bar shown and the column folded away, the page no longer covers the bar.
- A page that fails to load after a redirect shows the address that failed in the field.
- A pin in a split put away in the background no longer stays as a sleeping half.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.34-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.34.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
