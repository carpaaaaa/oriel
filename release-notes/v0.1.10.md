**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **Ctrl+Shift+P no longer seems to turn the page black and do nothing.** With See through the floating video turned all the way up, Oriel's floating window came out almost invisible. Now the floating video stays solid for its first moments, so you see where it went, before it fades.
- **The tab whose video floats in Oriel's window says so**, with a button to bring it back, instead of showing an empty dark page.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.10-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.10.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
