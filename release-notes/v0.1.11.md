**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **A private tab you can find.** Right-click Oriel's icon in the taskbar: New Window and New Private Tab. Ctrl+Shift+N makes one too, and a private tab's blank page now says "A tab that keeps nothing". Its eye shows in the column and across the top.
- **Always show the address bar** (Settings › Appearance › Window) works: a row with the page's full address, at the top of the column or before the tabs. Click it, or press Ctrl+L, to type there.
- **Show how far you've read** (Settings › Appearance › Tabs) works, in the column and across the top.
- **Split View is one thing in the tab list.** The two tabs sit in one frame. Hold Shift and drag a tab onto another to split them; without Shift, dragging only moves the tab.
- **A click outside lets go of picked tabs.** Ctrl-click to pick several, then click anywhere else (or press Esc) to put the picks down.
- **The floating video is one switch** (Settings › Browsing › Video). Its buttons wear Oriel's look and show only while the pointer is on the window, with a thin line to seek. See through still fades it away from the pointer, and a site's own picture-in-picture button floats the video the same way.

## Changed

- **Thin scrollbars.** A slim, quiet bar with no arrows, in step with the browser. A site that draws its own keeps it.
- **Borderless window keeps the taskbar within reach.** It covers the screen without telling Windows it is full screen, so a taskbar set to hide comes up when the pointer goes to its edge. With the taskbar always showing, the window fills what it leaves.
- **Themes:** Intensity (and Density and Scale for stickers) sit right under the choice of look, not under a long grid of walls.
- **Bounce at the top and bottom is gone.** On Windows a page only stretched under a precision touchpad, so the setting did nothing for most people.

## Fixed

- **Scroll with the middle button** kept working when it was turned off.
- **Electron's icon in the taskbar** for pop-ups a site opens and for developer tools: they carry Oriel's now.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.11-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.11.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
