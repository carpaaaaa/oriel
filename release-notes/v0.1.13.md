**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Split View by carrying a tab.** Hold Shift and carry a tab onto another, across the top or in the column, and the two go side by side. To do it without Shift, turn on **Settings › Tabs › Split View without Shift**: the middle of a tab is then the target, and its edges still move the tab among the others.
- **The same scroll bars on every site.** Thin, rounded and quiet, they now replace the ones a site draws for itself, and they are on Oriel's own pages too (What's New, Settings). **Settings › Pages › Scroll bars** brings Windows' own back.

## Fixed

- **Borderless window** asked Windows about the taskbar and about its own frame in a way that could take Oriel down when it went full screen; both are now asked once in a while and answered on Windows' own turn. A pixel of rounding on a scaled screen no longer counts as "past the screen".

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.13-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.13.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
