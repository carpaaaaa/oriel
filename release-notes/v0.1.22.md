**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Changed

- **Full screen is just full screen.** The Borderless window choice is gone from Settings: F11 and Ctrl+S take the page full screen, and the window covers its screen exactly, with no pixel short at any edge. Measured: the window, its frame and its client area all match the monitor.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.22-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.22.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
