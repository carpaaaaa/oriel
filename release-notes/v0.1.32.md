**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **No dark page left after signing in with Google.** Google's sign-in opens in a tab and closes itself once you are in, but Oriel didn't hear it ask: the tab stayed there, empty and dark. It now closes by itself and the page you were signing in to comes back in front. The same for any pop-up that closes itself when it is done, a payment's for one.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.32-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.32.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
