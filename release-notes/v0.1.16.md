**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Back and Forward are Ctrl+Backspace and Ctrl+Shift+Backspace**, in place of Alt+Left and Alt+Right. Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+] stay. Typing is never touched: in a field, the address bar or editable text, Ctrl+Backspace still deletes a word. Shortcuts you changed yourself in Settings are left as they are.
- **Stalled downloads.** A download with nothing coming in for ten seconds says **Stalled** in the list, and says Downloading again as soon as bytes come.

## Fixed

- **Borderless window, switched while in full screen:** choosing Borderless window in Settings while the window was already in full screen did nothing; the taskbar still wouldn't come up. It now changes at once, and the taskbar comes up at the bottom edge.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.16-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.16.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
