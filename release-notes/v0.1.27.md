**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Text size.** Settings › Appearance › Text size makes Oriel's own text bigger, from 100% to 140%: the tabs in every layout, menus, Settings, What's New and the other Oriel pages, as the slider moves. Reset takes it back to 100%. Websites keep their own size.
- **A star on GitHub, asked once after an update.** A small card in the corner of the page, never on a fresh install, never taking the keys, gone with its X, by itself after a while, or for good with Don't ask again.

## Changed

- **A video going full screen glides.** The page grows from where it sits to the whole screen over a darkening backdrop, and shrinks back into place on the way out. Electron's own full screen no longer moves the window first.
- **Lighter.** The installer is about 16 MB smaller, the interface code is more than half the size, and a page's own scripts load only when a feature is on: password autofill, videos waiting for a click, reading progress and the YouTube script stay out of the way otherwise. Idle timers (downloads, battery saver, the folded column's edge) run only when needed, a minimized window stops drawing, and the link preview card gives up its process when unused.

## Fixed

- **Downloads.** The progress in the card no longer jumps back and forth. A download isn't called Stalled the moment it resumes. One cut off by the network says Failed and why, with Resume, instead of Paused. Retry starts from the address the download began at, not the last redirect. Cancelling or removing an interrupted download ends it and removes its part-file.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.27-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.27.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
