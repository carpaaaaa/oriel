**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Clear all data when Oriel quits.** Turn it on in **Settings › Clear › Clear all data when Oriel quits** (it asks first). Each time you quit, Oriel erases the cookies and site data, the cache, the history, the saved passwords, the list of downloads, the closed tabs and the tabs it would bring back. Bookmarks, pins, spaces, Settings and the files you downloaded stay. If Oriel is closed without quitting (a crash, the power going), the next launch erases first.
- **Backspace goes back** (Settings › Keyboard & Mouse, off): Backspace goes back a page and Shift+Backspace forward, when you aren't typing in a field.
- **Return takes the top suggestion** (Settings › Browsing, off): the place on top of the address field's list is picked as you type, so Return goes to it instead of searching for the words.
- Following Search 1.0.21: closing tabs across the top one after another keeps their width while the pointer stays on the strip, so the next tab's cross comes to where the last one was.

## Fixed

- **LOCALHOST** and other local names typed in capitals open over HTTP, like `localhost`.
- **Darken pages** leaves Google Docs, Sheets and Slides as they are.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.15-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.15.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
