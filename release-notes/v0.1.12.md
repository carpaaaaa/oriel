**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## New

- **Install from a terminal.** Scoop works today: `scoop bucket add oriel https://github.com/carpaaaaa/scoop-oriel`, then `scoop install oriel`. winget and Chocolatey are on their way. Every release now lists the SHA-256 of each installer in SHA256SUMS.txt, so you can check a download.

## Fixed

- **Oriel no longer jumps back to the front** when you go to another app while the tab switcher is up (Ctrl held after Ctrl+Tab), or while a menu is closing.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.12-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.12.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
