**Oriel is Search, for Windows.** Installed copies update to this on their own.

## Fixed

- **The floating video's buttons answer a real click.** Close, back to the tab, back and forward 15 seconds and play or pause did nothing under a real mouse: Windows took the whole little window for a title bar, buttons included, and swallowed the click. The window is now moved by the page itself: hold the video anywhere but on a button and drag.
- **Back to the video is smoother.** The page used to drop its floating look while it was still the little window's size, so a player laid itself out small and then again at full size. It now waits until it is back at its own size.
- **The floating video asks less of the PC.** The see-through no longer keeps asking Windows where the pointer is, thirty times a second, once it has settled, and the window's place is saved when you let go of it, not at every step of a drag.

Oriel isn't code-signed yet, so updates are checked only by their checksum from GitHub; see "Security" in the README.

## Install

`Oriel-Setup-0.1.25-x64.exe` for most PCs, `…-arm64.exe` for Snapdragon PCs (`Oriel-Setup-0.1.25.exe` holds both). On "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
